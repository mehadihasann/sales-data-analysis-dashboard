# Sales Data Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end sales analytics project: from raw retail data to executive-ready insights and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Business question
Which products, categories, and regions actually drive revenue — and where should the business focus next?

## What I did
- **Exploratory analysis in Python** (`sales-dashboard-project/notebook/analysis.ipynb`): cleaned and explored the sales records with pandas, visualized trends with matplotlib.
- **Interactive dashboard in Power BI**: category, regional, and product-level views built for non-technical stakeholders.

## Key findings
- **Technology leads** — the Technology category contributes the largest share of total sales, indicating stronger demand and pricing power.
- **West region dominates** — the highest regional revenue, making the strongest case for further investment.
- **Seasonal Q4 peak** — sales trend upward overall, with a pronounced fourth-quarter spike consistent with year-end demand.
- **Concentration risk** — a small number of products drive a disproportionate share of revenue; diversifying would reduce dependency on key items.
